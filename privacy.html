Website Privacy Notice

Effective November 8, 2026

This notice covers the freefield.co website, operated by Freefield Labs Inc., a British Columbia corporation. For our app, see the Fairplay Privacy Policy.

The short version

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and it doesn’t load anything from third parties. There are no forms or accounts.

Server logs

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes basic request information, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. These logs are kept for a short time and aren’t used to identify or profile visitors. Vercel may process this information in the United States and other countries.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply and keep a record of the conversation. We don’t add you to mailing lists.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask a privacy question by emailing privacy@freefield.co. You can also contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (oipc.bc.ca).

Changes

If we change this notice, we’ll update it here with a new effective date.
