01/Our apps

Fairplay

iPhone · Free on the App Store

Post your plans, set the spots, and let your friends claim them.

Fairplay is the easiest way to get a plan off the group chat and onto the calendar. Create an activity (a tee time, a ski day, a hike, anything), set how many spots there are, and share it with friends or a group. People claim a spot, a waitlist forms behind them, and everyone’s notified when things change. Roster’s full? It’s on.

  • 01Spots, waitlist and alerts

    Set how many people can come. When the roster fills, a waitlist forms automatically, and everyone hears when someone joins or drops out.

  • 02Groups

    Keep your regular crews together and post a plan to a whole group at once, with optional approval for new members.

  • 03Message boards and meeting spots

    Every activity has its own message board, and the meeting-spot pin is shared only with the people who’ve joined.

  • 04Private friend finding

    Find friends from your contacts. Numbers are hashed on your phone before matching, and your address book is never stored.
